One Plugin Instead of One Per Gateway

The usual way to take payments on Joomla is to install a separate extension for every provider — one for PayPal, another for Stripe, another for whichever local provider your customers actually use. Each brings its own settings, its own update cycle and its own way of breaking. PaymentHood is a single plugin that reaches {{ site.provider_floor }} providers, so adding or switching a provider happens in a dashboard instead of in your extension manager.

Not sure which cart your site runs?

Open your Joomla administrator and go to Extensions → Manage → Manage, then look for one of these three components.

com_virtuemart

You are on VirtueMart. It adds its own top-level admin menu, and payment methods are configured under VirtueMart → Payment Methods.

com_phocacart

You are on Phoca Cart. Payment methods live under Components → Phoca Cart → Payment Methods.

com_j2store

You are on J2Commerce. The component still carries the J2Store name from before the rename, and payment plugins are listed under its Payment Methods screen.

Live in three steps

The standard Joomla install route — no FTP access and no file editing.

1

Download the plugin

Pick the plugin for your cart and download the ZIP. It is free, with no purchase step and no approval to wait on.

2

Install it in Joomla

Upload the ZIP through Extensions → Manage → Install, then paste your PaymentHood API credentials into the plugin settings.

3

Turn on your providers

Enable the providers you want from your PaymentHood dashboard. Adding another later does not touch your Joomla site at all.
