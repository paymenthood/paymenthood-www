Frequently asked questions

What's included in the free plan?

Everything needed to accept payments: all gateways, all platform integrations, unlimited transactions, analytics, fraud protection, and full API access. Free forever, no credit card required.

Are there any transaction fees?

Never, on either plan. PaymentHood takes no percentage and no per-transaction cut, at any volume. You pay only what your own payment provider charges you directly.

What does Premium add?

White-labeling, and nothing else. Every payment feature is already free. Premium simply lets you put your own logo, colors, domain, and wording on the checkout page and remove the PaymentHood badge.

Does the free plan show PaymentHood branding?

Yes. On the free plan your checkout page shows PaymentHood branding. Upgrading to Premium removes it completely and lets you apply your own design.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no commitments. Cancel your Premium subscription anytime and drop back to the free plan instantly.

How long does it take to migrate from my current payment gateway?

For WHMCS and WooCommerce and all our integration plugins, setup takes under 5 minutes, download, upload, activate. For custom integrations, our team provides a dedicated migration guide and free support at no extra cost.
