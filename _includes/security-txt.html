Do not inline this into either file — two hand-maintained copies drift, and a security contact that is wrong in one of them is worse than having none. Published at two URLs on purpose: /.well-known/security.txt — the location RFC 9116 requires; canonical. /security.txt — the legacy location. OpenBugBounty verifies domain ownership by fetching exactly this URL and matching the OpenBugBounty: line below, so it is a functional requirement, not a fallback. `OpenBugBounty:` is not a registered RFC 9116 field and conforming parsers must ignore it, which is why the same URL is also given as a `Policy:` — that is the registered field for "the terms we accept reports under", so standards-based tooling still finds the programme. Keep both lines: dropping the Policy hides it from tools, dropping the OpenBugBounty line breaks domain verification. `Acknowledgments:` is the RFC 9116 spelling — American, no middle "e". The British "Acknowledgements" appeared in the pre-RFC drafts and conforming parsers will not match it. It points at the hall of fame on the disclosure page, whose names come from _data/security_researchers.yml. Expires is REQUIRED and must be a future RFC 3339 timestamp. It is derived from build time plus one year so every deploy refreshes it; a hardcoded date would silently lapse and invalidate the file.
