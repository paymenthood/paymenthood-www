Cost

{{ d.name }} fees and pricing

Two separate bills, and only one of them exists.

What PaymentHood charges
Nothing on your {{ d.name }} transactions. No percentage and no per-transaction cut, at any volume. Every payment feature is free, and the optional Premium plan only adds white-label checkout branding.
What {{ d.name }} charges
{{ d.name }} sets its own rates and bills you directly. PaymentHood never sits between you and that invoice, so switching to or from {{ d.name }} changes what you pay {{ d.name }} and nothing else.
What moves the rate
Payment providers price per market and per method rather than as a single global figure. {%- if d.countries and d.countries.size > 1 %} {{ d.name }} operates across {{ d.countries.size }} countries, so the country the payment is taken in matters.{%- endif %} {%- if d.currencies and d.currencies.size > 1 %} It settles in {% for c in d.currencies %}{{ c }}{% unless forloop.last %}, {% endunless %}{% endfor %}, and cross-currency transactions are not priced the same as domestic ones.{%- endif %} {%- if d.methods and d.methods.size > 1 %} It also accepts {{ d.methods.size }} different payment methods, and card, transfer and wallet rails each carry their own cost.{%- endif %}
Where to check the figure
{%- if d.links.pricing != d.links.website %}{{ d.name }} publishes current rates on their pricing page (opens in new tab). {%- else %}{{ d.name }} publishes current rates on their own site (opens in new tab). {%- endif %} That is the authority, not this page. We deliberately quote no numbers here, because a rate that has quietly changed is worse than no rate at all.
