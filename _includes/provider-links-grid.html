For readers it answers the question the product page raises and does not answer — "yes, but does it do MY provider?". For crawlers it is the only internal path to the provider × platform pages; without it they are orphans and will not rank no matter how good they are. Sourced from _data/providers/*.yml: an entry appears only when its `status` is `live` AND its `platforms` list contains this platform. Providers still in development therefore never render, which is what keeps the page honest as new data files land.
