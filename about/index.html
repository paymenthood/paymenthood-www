The company behind the product

One Company Builds PaymentHood, and Stands Behind It

PaymentHood is a payment orchestration platform owned and operated by OmegaHood LLC. We build the product, we sell it, we invoice you, we support it, and we are accountable for it. There is no second company in your agreement and no ambiguity about who answers when you need something.

One product

PaymentHood is our product. Connect 30+ payment providers through a single integration and route every payment automatically.

One agreement

You contract with OmegaHood LLC. Our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy are the documents that govern your account.

One point of accountability

Security, support, uptime, billing, and claims all sit with OmegaHood LLC. You never have to work out which company to approach.

Legal & commercial

Your Agreement at a Glance

Exactly who you are dealing with, on every question that matters for procurement, finance, and legal review.

Contracting party
OmegaHood LLC. Your agreement for PaymentHood is with OmegaHood LLC, and with no other company.
Seller of record
OmegaHood LLC. We are the seller of the PaymentHood subscription, not a reseller or agent for anyone else.
Billing and invoicing
OmegaHood LLC issues every invoice and receipt for PaymentHood, and is the payee on your subscription.
Data controller
OmegaHood LLC is the controller for the account and service data described in our Privacy Policy. Any supplier that handles data on our behalf does so as a processor, under contract and on our instructions.
Cardholder data
Never held by us. Card details pass directly from your customer to your chosen payment provider's certified environment, and PaymentHood works only with the tokens returned. See the Trust Center.
Governing law
The laws of the State of California, United States, as set out in our Terms of Use, which is the authoritative document if anything on this page is less specific.
Support, security, and service levels
OmegaHood LLC. Every support request, security question, and service commitment is ours to answer.
Liability and claims
OmegaHood LLC, on the terms and limits set out in our Terms of Use.

Technology We Build On

Like any software company, we build on technology from others. PaymentHood uses APIs provided by InfyniPay B.V. (opens in new tab) (Amsterdam, Netherlands) under a commercial agreement, alongside the payment providers you connect and standard cloud infrastructure.

These are suppliers to OmegaHood LLC. They are not party to your agreement, they do not invoice you, and they do not provide your support. Your relationship, in every respect, is with OmegaHood LLC.
