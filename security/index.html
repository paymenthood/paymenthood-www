We never touch card data

PaymentHood does not store, process, or transmit cardholder data. Card details flow directly between your customer and your payment provider's certified infrastructure. PaymentHood orchestrates every payment by secure reference only, keeping sensitive card data out of our systems and yours.

Security Foundation

Industry-Standard Cryptography, Everywhere

Every layer of PaymentHood is protected with strong, proven cryptography: credentials at rest, traffic in transit, webhooks, and account access.

🔐

Encrypted Credential Storage

Every merchant's payment provider credentials are encrypted at rest with AES-256, using a dedicated key per merchant. No two merchants ever share an encryption boundary.

🗝️

Sealed Encryption Keys

The encryption keys themselves are sealed with AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption under a master key held in a managed key service. Keys are never stored alongside the data they protect.

📱

Enforceable MFA

Sensitive account actions are protected by TOTP-based multi-factor authentication (Google Authenticator and compatible apps). Administrators can make MFA mandatory across their entire team.

👥

Role-Based Team Access

Invite your team with role-based permissions, so every member is scoped to exactly what they need and nothing more.

✍️

Signed Webhooks

Every webhook is signed with HMAC-SHA256 and timestamped, so you can verify each notification genuinely came from PaymentHood and hasn't been tampered with or replayed.

🔒

Encrypted In Transit

All traffic between your systems, your customers, and PaymentHood is protected with HTTPS/TLS. End to end, with no exceptions.

Webhook Engine

Webhook Platform

PaymentHood keeps your systems in sync with a complete, reliable webhook engine that is secure by design and fully under your control.

🔁

Reliable Delivery

Events are queued and delivered asynchronously, with automatic retries whenever your endpoint is temporarily unavailable, so you never miss an event.

✅

Verified Authenticity

Every delivery is signed with HMAC-SHA256 and timestamped. Verify the signature with your secret to be certain the request came from PaymentHood, untampered and never replayed.

🎛️

You Control the Secret

Generate and rotate your signing secret at any time, or disable signing whenever you like. Need more? Add your own Authorization header for endpoints that require it.

🔎

Full Visibility

Inspect every delivery attempt: status, response code, errors, timestamps, and the exact payload. Filter by payment, reference, outcome, or date, and debug with confidence.

One Integration

Unified Payment Method Abstraction

Integrate once and work with a simple customer reference. Your systems deal only with a customer ID and an amount. PaymentHood handles everything behind it.

  • Chooses which saved card or bank account to charge
  • Handles tokenization and the underlying payment gateway
  • Routes by currency and runs fraud checks automatically
  • Add or swap providers without changing your integration
Traceability

Every Action, On the Record

PaymentHood logs the full lifecycle of every payment: every status change, when it happened, who triggered it, and why. We also record every gateway interaction and every webhook delivery attempt.

  • Complete payment lifecycle: every status change, actor, and reason
  • Every gateway interaction and webhook delivery attempt
  • Stored in secure cloud logging (Microsoft Azure) with managed retention
  • End-to-end tracing whenever something needs investigating
Sandbox

A Sandbox That Mirrors Reality

Test against your real provider, not a mock. Plus a built-in test provider when you want zero setup.

🌐

Your Real Provider's Sandbox

PaymentHood's sandbox isn't a simulation of a gateway. It connects straight to your payment provider's own test environment (Adyen test, PayFort/Amazon sandbox, and every other supported gateway) using your provider test credentials. You see the exact behavior, responses, and edge cases of your live provider, with no real money moving. What you validate in sandbox is what you get in production.

⚡

Built-In Test Provider

Prefer to skip provider setup entirely? PaymentHood ships a built-in test provider: run the full authorize → capture → refund flow, approve or decline at will, and receive real signed webhooks, without configuring any gateway.

  • 💳 Test cards
  • 🧪 Built-in test provider
  • 🔔 Webhook simulation
  • ⚠️ Error simulation
  • ⏱️ Latency simulation

Sandbox and live are fully isolated: separate credentials, separate configuration, no crossover. Build against sandbox, then go live by swapping credentials. No code changes.
